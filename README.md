## 🌟 Executive Overview
**SupportSystem** is a dedicated web platform built for individuals seeking private 1-to-1 personal mentorship, life guidance, emotional grounding, career clarity, and stress management. 

The website provides a warm, confidential, non-judgmental environment where users can learn about Siddhi Patel’s mentoring methodology, book sessions with instant automated Zoom & calendar integration, engage in encrypted text messaging, and participate in a safe anonymous peer community.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. 📅 Seamless 1-to-1 Booking & Auto-Zoom Delivery
* **4-Step Booking Wizard**:
  * **Step 1: Session & Mode Selection**: Choose between Zoom Video or Zoom Audio Call, and select session duration (30 min, 45 min, 60 min, or 4-Week Transformation).
  * **Step 2: Confidential Intake**: Collects mentee profile (name, age, language preferences: Hindi/Gujarati/English/Hinglish, key challenges such as stress, career confusion, relationships, or life decisions).
  * **Step 3: Slot Selection & Instant Payment**: Selection of available time slots (IST) and payment via UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), Cards, or Net Banking.
  * **Step 4: Automated Zoom & Multi-Channel Dispatch**:
    * **Automated Zoom Link Generation**: Generates a dedicated Zoom meeting link, Meeting ID, and Passcode.
    * **Automated Email Dispatch**: Sends confirmation with the Zoom invitation and preparation guidelines directly to the user’s email.
    * **Automated WhatsApp Dispatch**: Sends an instant WhatsApp message with the meeting link and time reminder.
    * **1-Click Google Calendar & .ICS Sync**: Direct button to add the event to Google Calendar or download an `.ics` file.
